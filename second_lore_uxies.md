# 🌑 The Hunt — Second Lore
## Uxies & The Reverse Dream

---

> *"He understood the machine the moment it used him.*
> *Now he had to become it."*

---

## Who Is Uxies

**Name:** Uxies
**Origin:** Earth 2
**Race:** Virden — born from the mixed civilization of humans and the Fallen (Balian's followers)
**Gender:** Female
**DNA anomaly:** Carries a microscopic fragment of **Zalian's DNA** embedded in her Virden genome

She is Fridan's exact mirror:

```
FRIDAN                          UXIES
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Earth 1 human                   Earth 2 Virden
Carries Balian DNA fragment     Carries Zalian DNA fragment
Selected by Earth 2             (Not yet selected by anyone)
Used as a bridge to Earth 1     Could be used as bridge to Earth 2
Exploited without his knowing   Still unaware of what she carries
Remembers the dream             Has not yet dreamed the dream
Lost connection to Zalian       Has never had one — but feels something
```

---

## Uxies' Life Among the Virden

She lives in Earth 2's civilization — a world of **pure chaos**. War, competition, constant upheaval. The Virden are powerful, fast-evolving, and shaped by Balian's direct presence in their lives.

Uxies is different. Not visibly. Not in any way her people can name. But she has always felt **slightly misaligned** with the world around her.

- In a civilization built on chaos, she seeks stillness
- Among beings who can feel Balian's presence as heat and noise, she feels something else — a **faint, cool signal** she has no word for
- She doesn't understand it. The Virden have no concept of Zalian as a communicating presence — Zalian is sealed behind the Barrier, and the Virden know her only as a myth, a name Balian uses with a weight he never explains
- Uxies has, at times, **heard something in her sleep** — not a voice, not instructions — more like a frequency. A tone that exists just below the threshold of dreaming. She has never mentioned it to anyone.

That frequency is the Zalian fragment in her DNA. It's not receiving a transmission — Zalian doesn't know Uxies exists. It's simply the resonant hum of a divine signal trapped in a body that shouldn't contain it.

Uxies has spent her life feeling like she belongs to something she cannot name. She is right. She just doesn't know the name yet.

## How Fridan Finds Out About Uxies

Fridan doesn't start with Uxies. He starts with the **mechanism.**

After he wakes from the dream — transformed, hunted, severed from Zalian — he spends Act 2 piecing together how Earth 2 reached him. He has:

1. **Virden knowledge from the sphere transfer** — an ancient, instinctive understanding of how the dream infiltration system works, not as a memory but as a *feeling he can now think through*
2. **Information from the Second Faction** — partial, manipulative, but containing real technical truths they needed him to believe
3. **His own experience as the subject** — he *felt* how the dream was constructed. The red rail. The building. The scripted warmth of Zalian's voice layered over something else.

He reverse-engineers the machine from the inside:

> *They found him because he had a Balian fragment.*
> *They used that fragment as an anchor.*
> *They threaded the signal through Zalian's dream channel.*
> *Zalian became the relay — without knowing it.*

The logical inversion is immediate:

> *If a Balian fragment in an Earth 1 human was an anchor for Earth 2's signal —*
> *Then a Zalian fragment in an Earth 2 Virden would be an anchor for his signal.*
> *And if Zalian was the relay for their transmission —*
> *Then Uxies' Zalian fragment could be the relay for his message to Zalian.*

He doesn't know Uxies exists yet. He only knows that **someone like Uxies must exist** — the symmetry of the system demands it. Two gods. Two worlds. Two fragments.

**How he finds her:**
This is a plot point to develop — options:
- The Second Faction, in their manipulation, accidentally revealed that Earth 2 ran a mirror scan of its own population looking for Zalian fragments — to understand their own vulnerability. They found one. They buried the information.
- Fridan extracts this buried data — either by deceiving the Second Faction, or through a contact he develops in Act 2
- The Virden knowledge transferred through the sphere may include an instinctive *directional sense* — the Balian fragment in him can feel the pull of another divine signal, the way a compass feels north
- Or Uxies finds *him* first — drawn by the same logic, having decoded her own anomaly

## The Plan — Reverse Engineering the Dream

Fridan's plan is elegant in its irony: **he will do to Uxies exactly what was done to him.**

He will:
1. **Locate Uxies** — confirm she exists and carries the Zalian fragment
2. **Build a transmission** — using the Virden knowledge in him to reconstruct a version of Earth 2's dream infrastructure, crude but functional
3. **Infiltrate her REM sleep** — thread his signal through the Zalian fragment in her DNA, using it as an anchor point, exactly as Earth 2 used his Balian fragment
4. **Place a message in her dream** — not instructions, not a Hunt. Something her Zalian fragment will amplify and carry upward, toward Zalian
5. **Use Uxies as the relay** — just as Zalian was used as a relay, unknowingly, to reach Earth 1

The difference in intent: Fridan is not extracting data. He is delivering a **warning.**

But the mechanism is identical. The exploitation is identical. Uxies does not consent. She will not know.

---

## The Secrecy Problem

This is the most dangerous part of the plan. Fridan is being watched by **two factions:**

### From Earth 2's civilization:
- They have surveillance on Fridan — he is a loose end, a breach in their system
- Any attempt to access a dream transmission channel will be detectable
- If they discover he is trying to reach Uxies, they will either stop him or use the connection themselves
- **Fridan must not build or use any infrastructure that resembles their own** — he must find a different frequency, a different approach

### From the Second Faction:
- They told him Earth 2 is peaceful — they need him to believe this
- If Fridan contacts Uxies, the Second Faction may lose their leverage over him
- They may also want Uxies for their own purposes — a Virden with Zalian DNA is extraordinarily valuable to any party trying to breach the Barrier
- **Fridan must not speak his plan aloud** — the Second Faction has ways of monitoring what he says, and Zalian's speech-only constraint (she can only read what is spoken) cuts both ways: anyone listening to *him* only needs to hear him

### The Zalian constraint:
- If Fridan reaches Zalian through Uxies, Zalian will receive the message — but she cannot know it came from Fridan directly, because she severed contact with him
- She may reject or ignore anything that feels like his signal
- The message must reach her as something that feels like **Uxies' own dream** — not a transmission, but an organic thought from a being she doesn't yet know carries her fragment
- Fridan must hide inside Uxies' dream so completely that even Zalian cannot tell he was there

## The Moral Weight

Fridan knows what it felt like to be used.

He remembers the apartment in the dream — the watchers, the questions, the smokescreen. He remembers thinking he was doing the right thing. He remembers the sphere felt like a gift.

He knows now it was an extraction.

And he is about to do the same thing to Uxies.

This is the ethical core of the Second Lore:

> *Is it still wrong if the intent is to protect?*
> *Does Uxies deserve to know what she's being used for?*
> *What happens to her when she wakes up — like he did — carrying something she was never supposed to carry?*

Fridan has a choice he didn't have:

- He can **warn Uxies first** — contact her as himself, explain the plan, ask for her consent. But this risks exposure, requires crossing the Barrier in some form, and puts her in immediate danger from both factions.
- He can **use her without telling her** — do to her what was done to him. The warning reaches Zalian. Uxies wakes up changed. And Fridan has become the machine that broke him.

The story does not resolve this question easily. **That is the point.**

## What Uxies Experiences

If Fridan succeeds without her knowing:

- Uxies has an unusually vivid dream — not structured like a Hunt, but emotional, vast, like dreaming of a voice that knows her name but won't show a face
- She wakes with a feeling she cannot describe — something between recognition and loss
- The frequency she has always heard in her sleep is **slightly louder now**
- She does not remember the content of the dream. Only the feeling.
- She begins looking for the source of the signal. She doesn't know she is now looking for Fridan.

If Fridan's transmission is detected:

- Earth 2's surveillance flags an anomaly in Uxies' REM pattern
- They begin monitoring her
- She is now in danger — a Virden with Zalian DNA who has been contacted from outside is either an asset or a threat
- The Second Faction moves to intercept her before Earth 2 does
- Uxies — who only just began to understand what she carries — is now hunted from two sides
- She and Fridan must find each other for real, in waking life (or waking-equivalent), to finish what the dream started

---

## The Central Dramatic Question

> **How does Fridan infiltrate Uxies' dreams without Earth 2 detecting it, without the Second Faction intercepting it, without Zalian recognizing it as him — and do it in a way that leaves Uxies intact, not destroyed by what she carries?**

The answer will require:
- A transmission built from **Virden instinct, not Earth 2 technology** (so it doesn't match their signature)
- Disguising the message as a **native resonance of the Zalian fragment** — not a signal from outside, but something that feels like it grew from within Uxies herself
- Timing it during a **natural REM window** where Earth 2's surveillance is focused elsewhere
- Encoding the warning in **symbolic, not linguistic form** — something Zalian will understand but that looks like a dream, not a message

And underneath all of that:

> *Can Fridan do this without becoming the thing he hated?*
