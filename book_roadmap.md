**About the Second Faction:**
- Who are they — a third civilization, a rogue Earth 2 group, an ancient Earth 1 secret society?

## Phase 2 — First Draft (Plain Text)

**Goal:** Write the full story — rough, complete, imperfect. No editing while drafting.

### Chapter Breakdown (~30 Chapters)

#### Act 1 — The Dream (Chapters 1–10)
*Present tense. Visceral, immersive, fast.*

| Chapter | Scene |
|---------|-------|
| 1 | Cold open: the Dreamer inside the dream. The Hunt has already begun. We don't know yet that it's a dream. |
| 2 | The first building. The exotic, intimidating people. The first clue. The Dreamer's certainty he can find it. |
| 3 | Zalian's voice appears for the first time. Scripted, warm, constrained. She doesn't explain — she guides. |
| 4 | The red train rail. Reality bending. Following it to the next location. The city at night. |
| 5 | Entering the second building. The group waiting for him. The apartment. "You should rest." |
| 6 | The Dreamer ignores rest — begins searching. The watchers observe. Suspicious questions begin. |
| 7 | Zalian gives another clue. The Dreamer senses something is off about the watchers — they know too much. |
| 8 | Close. Almost found. The tension of the smokescreen fracturing. Who are these people *really*? |
| 9 | He finds the Sphere. The sensory explosion — goosebumps, the Hans Zimmer chord, the ear pressure. The brain expansion. |
| 10 | He wakes. He remembers everything. The timestamp: 08/26/26 — New Jersey. The system failed. |

| Chapter | Scene |

| 14 | The Second Faction makes contact. They know about the dream. They're not surprised he remembered. |
| 15 | The Dreamer learns: others have done this before him. None of them remembered. |
| 16 | Earth 2 agents appear — trying to contain the breach. The world becomes dangerous. |
| 17 | Discovery: his DNA. A fragment that shouldn't be there. What does it mean? |

### Writing Rules
1. **Write ugly.** The first draft's only job is to exist.
2. **Use placeholders:** `[FILL: describe Zalian's appearance]` — keep moving.
3. **Chapter 1 is already written** — it's Fridan's dream log, shaped into prose.
4. **Target:** 60,000–80,000 words. ~2,000–2,500 words per chapter.
5. **Daily habit:** 500 words/day = first draft in 4–5 months.

### Recommended Writing Tools
| Tool | Best For |
|------|----------|
| **Scrivener** | Industry standard. Manages chapters, notes, research side-by-side |
| **Notion** | Free, flexible, great for the Story Bible + draft together |
| **Obsidian** | If you like linked notes and want a wiki-style world map |
| **Google Docs** | Simple, autosaves, shareable |

**Estimated time:** 3–6 months

## Phase 3 — Illustrated Edition (Text + Spot Illustrations)

**Goal:** Polish the text and place strategic illustrations at key moments.

### Priority Illustrations (in order)

| # | Subject | Why First |
|---|---------|-----------|
| 1 | **The Sphere** with symbol `(: .)` | The most iconic visual in the story — establish it early |
| 2 | **The Cosmic Hierarchy diagram** | Readers need a visual map of Earth 1 / Earth 2 / The Barrier / the two hybrids |
| 3 | **Zalian** — her dream appearance | The most emotionally important character |
| 4 | **The Barrier** — visualized as biological incompatibility, not a wall | Abstract but essential |
| 5 | **The Hunt Arena** — the buildings, the night, the red rail | The dream setting |
| 6 | **Fridan** — a character portrait | Root the reader in a face |
| 7 | **Earth 2 / The Virden** — a glimpse of the chaotic civilization | Contrast with Earth 1 |
| 8 | **Balian** — not as a villain, as an exile | His emotional complexity deserves a visual |
| 9 | **The mirror-Virden** — alone in Earth 2, feeling something different | The Second Lore's human hook |

### How to Get Illustrations

| Option | Cost | Best For |
|--------|------|----------|
| **AI generation** (Midjourney, DALL-E) | Free–\$10/mo | Rapid concept exploration, your own vision |
| **Hire an illustrator** (Fiverr, ArtStation) | \$50–\$500/piece | Final polished pieces |
| **Procreate (iPad)** | \$13 one-time | Full creative control if you want to draw yourself |

> [!TIP]
> Use AI generation now to build your visual reference library — what does Zalian feel like? What does Earth 2 look like? Collect images that *feel right*, even if they're not final art. This gives future illustrators a precise brief.

**Estimated time:** 2–3 months
